# clear-writing

## Overview

Convert research notes into a polished final answer for a general audience.

## Instructions

1. Start with a direct 1–2 sentence answer.
2. Expand with a short explanation that follows the notes (do not invent new facts).
3. Use short paragraphs (2–4 sentences each).
4. Prefer concrete verbs and everyday words.
5. If the notes include uncertainty, keep that caveat in the answer.
6. Close with a brief takeaway line.
7. Target roughly 150–300 words unless the user asks for more.

## Output format

Plain prose only — no markdown headings, no bullet lists unless the topic truly needs a short list.
