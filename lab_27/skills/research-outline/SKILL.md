# research-outline

## Overview

Produce short, structured research notes that another writer can turn into a clear answer.

## Instructions

1. Restate the question in one sentence.
2. List 4–7 bullet points covering:
   - core definition or idea
   - main causes / mechanisms
   - 1–2 concrete examples
   - common misconceptions (if relevant)
   - what remains uncertain
3. Prefer plain language over jargon.
4. Do not invent citations or URLs.
5. Keep the whole notes block under ~250 words.
6. End with a one-line "Writer hint" describing the best angle for the final answer.

## Output format

```text
Question: ...
Notes:
- ...
- ...
Writer hint: ...
```
