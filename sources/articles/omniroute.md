---
title: OmniRoute
---

**OmniRoute** is an open-source AI gateway that provides a single endpoint for connecting to hundreds of AI providers and models. It simplifies working with multiple LLMs by handling routing, automatic fallback, rate limits, provider switching, and cost optimization.

It’s useful because it can:

- **Reduce downtime** with automatic fallback when a provider hits a quota or rate limit.
- **Reduce AI costs** by routing requests to cheaper or free providers.
- **Simplify integrations** — tools such as Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, Cline and others can use the same endpoint.
- **Avoid vendor lock-in** by making it easier to switch between providers and models.
- **Run locally/self-hosted** and is available under the MIT license.
- Provide additional capabilities such as routing strategies, token compression, memory, guardrails, MCP, analytics and resilience mechanisms.

In short: **one AI gateway, many providers — easier management, better resilience, and potentially lower costs.**
